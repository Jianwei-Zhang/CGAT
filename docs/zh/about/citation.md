# 引用与反馈

## GPM

使用 GPM 的可视化 pseudomolecule 构建思想与相关工作时，请引用：

> Zhang, J. et al. Genome Puzzle Master (GPM): an integrated pipeline for building and editing pseudomolecules from fragmented sequences. *Bioinformatics* 32(20), 3058–3064 (2016). [https://doi.org/10.1093/bioinformatics/btw370](https://doi.org/10.1093/bioinformatics/btw370)

## DEGAP

使用 DEGAP 时，请引用：

> Huang, Y. et al. DEGAP: Dynamic Elongation of a Genome Assembly Path. *Briefings in Bioinformatics* 25(3), bbae194 (2024). [https://doi.org/10.1093/bib/bbae194](https://doi.org/10.1093/bib/bbae194)
